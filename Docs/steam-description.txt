[b]All the Progression goodness, less research.[/b]

[i]This is a fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819707781]Progression: Bionics[/url] by ferny. Huge thanks to ferny for the original mod and all the Progression work this builds on. For a really good modpack, check out [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3521297585]The Progression Modpack[/url].[/i]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Overhauls bionics research, so every prosthetic, bionic, implant and organ has a place in the research tree.
[*]Prosthetics come before bionics, and bionics before advanced bionics: each upgrade still needs the part it improves on.
[*]Covers everything from hook hands and steel arms to synthetic organs, skill chips, modular bionics, genetic implants, android parts and mechanitor implants, for colonists and animals alike.
[/list]

[h2]Why "LESS"?[/h2]
Progression: Bionics adds up to [b]562 research projects[/b] depending on your mods. This fork groups them into [b]at most 79[/b], most of them on their own Bionics research tab, with the Anomaly projects on the Anomaly tab:
[list]
[*][b]Medieval:[/b] Medieval prosthetics, Golem modules
[*][b]Industrial:[/b] Prosthetic parts, Surrogate organs, Specialized limbs, Compact weapons, Brain wiring implants, Animal prosthetics, Animal prosthetic parts, Animal combat implants, Cybertech kits, Advanced cybertech kits
[*][b]Bionics:[/b] Bionic body parts, Bionic organs, Advanced bionic parts, Advanced scyther limbs, Power arm, Gravship implants, Shoulder weapons
[*][b]Spacer implants:[/b] Flesh shaping, Healing, Molecular, Neural and Circadian implants, Skin glands, Metabolic stomachs, Skill chips, Sanguophage implants
[*][b]Synthetic organs:[/b] Synthetic organ set, Rib implants, Glitterworld implants
[*][b]Modular bionics:[/b] Melee, Ranged weapon, Grenade and trap, Deployer, Projector, Mobility, Work, Utility, Power, Synthesizer, Orbital, Chemical burst and Ultratech modules, Subpersona chips
[*][b]Integrated Implants modules:[/b] Arm, Head, and Leg and lung modules, Crypto claw module, plus the seven modules Bionic modularity replaces when it is active
[*][b]Animals:[/b] Animal bionics, Animal bionic parts, Animal bionic implants, Animal natural weapons, Animal body implants, Animal work chips, Animal strike and producer modules, Animal mind controller
[*][b]Genetics, androids and hacking:[/b] Human genetic implants, Animal genetic implants, Android parts, Exec data programs
[*][b]Mechanitor:[/b] Signal boosters, Basic, High and Ultra mechanitor implants, Repair probe
[*][b]Anomaly:[/b] Ghoul enhancements, Advanced ghoul enhancements, Anomaly modules, Animal mutation implants, Arm incinerator module
[/list]
Everything else unlocks with the research it already builds on:
[list]
[*]Integrated Implants' implants: [b]Basic implants[/b], [b]Bionic tails[/b], [b]Biomimicry[/b], [b]Eltex augmentation[/b], [b]Emergency implants[/b], [b]Field manipulation implants[/b], [b]Subdermal armor[/b], [b]Psychic implants[/b] and [b]Ultratech arm modules[/b]
[*]EPOE-Forked parts: [b]Synthetic repair[/b], [b]Optimizing nanobots[/b], [b]Synthetic organ integration[/b], [b]Bionic compact weapons[/b] and [b]Advanced specialized limbs[/b]
[*]Bionic modularity's immunizers: [b]Mechanite modules[/b]
[*]Dead man's switches: [b]Advanced dead man's switches[/b]
[*]Glittertech teeth and skin: [b]Teeth replacement[/b] and [b]Skin replacement[/b]
[*]Detoxifier lung: [b]Toxin filtration[/b]; hygiene enhancer: [b]Hygiene bionics[/b]
[*]Revenant vertebrae: [b]Revenant invisibility[/b]; neural receiver: [b]Neural casting[/b]; memory projector: [b]Deep mind scanning[/b]; advanced cyberlink: [b]Advanced cybertech[/b]; War Crimes Expanded 2 kits: their own research
[/list]
Parts from different mods share a project when they share a family and a stage, so a bionic eye from one mod unlocks alongside the bionic legs and spines of others.

Everything stays in the same era, and still needs the other research it did before: a bionic hand still needs the prosthetic hand, and each weapon module still needs its weapon research.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Keeps all of the original's mod support:
[list]
[*]Royalty, Biotech, Anomaly and Odyssey
[*]Expanded Prosthetics and Organ Engineering - Forked and its Royalty expansion
[*]Integrated Implants, Bionic modularity and Bionic modularity - animals
[*]Alpha Implants, Alpha Mechs, Vanilla Genetics Expanded (and More Lab Stuff), Vanilla Races Expanded - Android
[*]Ushanka's Hacking, Glittertech and Biological Warfare expansions
[*]Pyrinth Bionics, Epochs - Golems, Big and Small, Altered Carbon 2 ReSleeved, Cellcycle Acceleration, Chimera Creation, Die for me, Dubs Bad Hygiene, Faction - Orbiters, Mechanoid Invaders, Royalty eye bionic, Security Clearance and War Crimes Expanded 2
[/list]

[b]Replaces the original. Don't use both.[/b]

[b]Switching mid-game isn't recommended.[/b] Many of the original's research projects are merged or removed, so swapping Progression: Bionics for this fork in an existing save can lose research progress. Start a new game when you switch.
